# Playlist.Kind

The available kinds of playlists.

iOS 15.0+iPadOS 15.0+Mac Catalyst 15.0+macOS 12.0+tvOS 15.0+visionOS 1.0+watchOS 8.0+

enum Kind

## [Topics](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum#topics)

### [Enumeration Cases](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum#Enumeration-Cases)

[`case editorial`](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum/editorial)

Indicates that the playlist was created by an Apple Music curator.

[`case external`](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum/external)

Indicates that the playlist was created by an external curator.

[`case personalMix`](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum/personalmix)

Indicates that the playlist is a personalized playlist for an Apple Music user.

[`case replay`](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum/replay)

Indicates that the playlist is a personalized Replay playlist for an Apple Music user.

[`case userShared`](/documentation/musickit/playlist/kind-swift.enum/usershared)

Indicates that the playlist was created and shared by an Apple Music user.
